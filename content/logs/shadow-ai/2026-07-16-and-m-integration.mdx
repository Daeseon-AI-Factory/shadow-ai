## Why the merge needed its own acceptance pass

The Android readiness track and the menu track were both valid in isolation, but they changed the same
tab layout for different reasons. One added measured bottom insets and Android-specific symbol weights;
the other established Home · Shadow · Speaking · Write · Me and updated the custom center tab for Expo
Router 56. Choosing either side of the conflict wholesale would silently drop a requirement from the
other track.

The merged tree also exposed two gaps that neither branch's happy-path result covered. The newly promoted
Shadow screen still contained eleven raw colors, and Home disappeared behind a full-screen error whenever
the initial profile or streak summary failed. Once Practice and Review are hidden tabs, that error state
also removes their only primary entry points.

## What the candidate preserves

The combined tab layout keeps the Android font modules, focus remount key, and measured safe-area inset,
while retaining the five visible destinations and Router 56 selected-state accessibility. Practice and
Review remain real hidden routes. Compose remains `/compose` after moving into the tab group. Home keeps
its streak, mastery summary, and speaking hero, with Review first and the full Practice library below.

Shadow's buttons, segmented control, cards, placeholders, and secondary text now consume semantic theme
tokens. Home no longer removes its navigation when summary data fails: an unavailable due count becomes a
generic Review action instead of a false zero, while the stable hub remains usable.
